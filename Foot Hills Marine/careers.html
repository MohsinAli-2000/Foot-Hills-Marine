Our culture

A collaborative, high-achieving environment

At Foothills Marine, we believe our dealership is only as exceptional as the team we cultivate. We provide a supportive atmosphere where your expertise is recognized, your ideas are encouraged, and your growth is funded.

Whether you are tuning advanced high-horsepower outboards in the service bay or helping a family build their custom dream pontoon, you will play a critical role in bringing high-performance recreation to the Carolinas.
