Our service department

Service you can rely on

The credibility of our Service Department is absolutely astounding. Specializing in stock and high performance Godfrey, Hurricane, Mako, Mercury, Nitro, Phoenix, Regency, Skeeter, Sportsman, Sun Tracker, Tahoe, Tidewater, Tracker, and Yamaha boats, we are equipped to make your boat suitable for you and your taste. We have top factory-trained technicians on duty during all business hours.

We honor all warranty work and favor any recalls. We accommodate any type of problem that may arise with your boat in an efficient, precise, and timely manner.

Foothills Marine Center is conveniently located near the areas of Hickory, Lenoir, Conover Newton, and Statesville.
