Morganton Service

Serving the Morganton Community

Our Morganton dealership is strategically located on N. Green Street to serve the Burke County and foothills area. It is a full-service center that makes buying, cleaning, and maintaining your watercraft convenient for residents across the region.

We feature a great lineup of new pontoons, deck boats, and fishing models, plus a highly responsive service center specializing in Mercury and Yamaha outboards. This location is also the home hub for our specialized mobile service team, bringing certified marine maintenance right to your marina slip.
