About Foothills Marine

Anglers selling to anglers. Explore our journey, our family-owned values, and our commitment to the Carolinas.

Our history

Established in 2001

Foothills Marine has been proudly serving boaters across the North Carolina foothills since 2001. We are a family-owned dealership passionate about getting people on the water and making memories that last a lifetime.

What started as a modest local shop has expanded into a premier regional resource with two state-of-the-art facilities. We handle everything from factory-authorized sales of the top brands to in-house professional rigging, certified service, and a fully stocked parts department.
