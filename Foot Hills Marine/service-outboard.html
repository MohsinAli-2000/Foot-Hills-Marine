Comprehensive systems maintenance

Our outboard services

Engine Diagnostics & Tune-ups

State-of-the-art computer fault-code reading, spark plug replacement, throttle calibration, and complete fuel performance sweeps.

Oil Changes & Filter Replacement

Four-stroke engine oil changes, premium OEM filter replacements, and meticulous lower-unit oil inspection for maximum protection.

Impeller & Water Pump Service

Essential cooling system maintenance. Replacement of water pump impellers and gaskets to prevent critical overheating failures.

Lower Unit Service & Repair

Pressure testing seals, propeller shaft runout inspection, gear oil replenishment, and complete gearcase rebuilds.

Electrical System Troubleshooting

Battery health diagnostics, starter motor testing, alternator output evaluation, and complete rewiring of critical helm accessories.

Propeller Repair & Replacement

Inspection for pitch distortions, blade reconditioning, hub bushing replacements, and performance propeller matching consultations.

Fuel System Service

VST tank cleaning, fuel injector sonic cleaning, filter replacements, and fuel water separator maintenance against ethanol fuel scaling.

Annual Maintenance Packages

Complete 100-hour or 300-hour factory-specified service packages ensuring compliance with all manufacturer warranty protocols.
