Authorized partner

Built to fish, made to last

Since 2003, Sportsman Boats has built its reputation on one core principle: designing and manufacturing premium saltwater fishing boats right here in the USA. With advanced hull designs, superior fit and finish, and a relentless focus on fishability, Sportsman delivers boats that perform in the most demanding offshore and inshore conditions.

As North Carolina's trusted authorized Sportsman dealer, Foothills Marine stocks and supports the entire lineup across our Mooresville and Morganton showrooms, offering full warranty support, custom rigging, and expert service.

Proven performance

Why anglers choose Sportsman

SUPERIOR FISHABILITY

Sportsman boats are designed from the keel up for serious fishing, with integrated rod holders, massive livewells, insulated fish boxes, and clean casting decks.

ADVANCED HULL DESIGN

Proprietary variable-degree deadrise hulls deliver a dry, stable ride in rough offshore seas while maintaining excellent shallow-water draft for inshore versatility.

PREMIUM CONSTRUCTION

100% composite, wood-free construction with hand-laid fiberglass and closed-cell foam flotation ensures decades of reliable, rot-free performance.

INDUSTRY-LEADING WARRANTY

Enjoy peace of mind with Sportsman's transferable limited lifetime hull warranty, fully backed and serviced in-house by our certified techs.
