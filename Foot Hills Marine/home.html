About the dealer

Thirty-three years of
Carolina water

Foothills Marine Center opened in Morganton in 1993 with a handful of fishing boats and a service bay. Today the same family runs an 80-boat climate-controlled showroom there, plus Foothills Marine of Lake Norman in Mooresville.

We are anglers selling to anglers. That means honest rigging advice, techs who fish the same lakes you do, and a trail of tournaments we put our name on every season.
